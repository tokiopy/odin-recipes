Habichuelas con Dulce

Home

Description

Habichuelas con dulce is a traditional Dominican dessert made with red beans, milk, sugar, and warm spices. It is usually served during Lent and enjoyed cold or warm with sweet crackers.

Ingredients

  • Red beans
  • Evaporated milk
  • Coconut milk
  • Sugar
  • Cinnamon
  • Cloves
  • Sweet potatoes
  • Raisins
  • Vanilla
  • Salt
  • Sweet crackers

Steps

  1. Cook the red beans until they are soft.
  2. Blend the beans with some of the cooking liquid.
  3. Strain the blended beans into a pot.
  4. Add evaporated milk, coconut milk, sugar, cinnamon, cloves, vanilla, and salt.
  5. Add the sweet potatoes and cook until they are soft.
  6. Stir often until the mixture becomes creamy.
  7. Add raisins near the end of cooking.
  8. Serve warm or cold with sweet crackers.
