Description

Dominican sancocho is a hearty stew made with different meats, root vegetables, plantains, and flavorful seasonings. It is a traditional dish often served during family gatherings and special occasions.

Ingredients

  • Beef
  • Chicken
  • Pork
  • Green plantains
  • Yuca
  • Yautia
  • Pumpkin
  • Corn
  • Cilantro
  • Oregano
  • Garlic
  • Salt
  • Water

Steps

  1. Season the meats with garlic, oregano, and salt.
  2. Brown the meats in a large pot.
  3. Add water and let the meats cook until they start to soften.
  4. Peel and cut the root vegetables and plantains.
  5. Add the vegetables, plantains, pumpkin, and corn to the pot.
  6. Cook everything until the vegetables are soft and the broth is thick.
  7. Add cilantro and adjust the salt if needed.
  8. Serve hot with white rice or avocado.
